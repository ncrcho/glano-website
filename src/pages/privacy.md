**Last updated: November 18, 2026**

Glano ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to help you organize and clean up your photo and video library while keeping your personal media on your device.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Glano handles and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Glano does not collect or transmit your personal data to us.**

The app does not operate its own servers and does not send information to Glano or any third party.

Glano processes your photos and videos locally on your iPhone. This includes identifying duplicate photos, similar images, screenshots, and large videos.

The app may store certain information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

- App preferences, such as appearance and reminder settings.
- Scan results, including item counts, file sizes, and references to matching photos.
- Deletion history, including file names, sizes, dimensions, dates, and other item details.
- Technical scan information used to help identify duplicates more efficiently.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Glano.

## 2. Photos and Videos

Glano requires access to your photo library to identify and review photos and videos.

All photo and video analysis takes place on your iPhone using Apple's built-in technologies. Your media is not uploaded to Glano or shared with third-party services by the app.

If you use iCloud Photos, some full-resolution photos or videos may be downloaded from your personal iCloud library to your device when you review them. This is handled through Apple's services.

When you delete an item through Glano, iOS handles the deletion. Deleted items are moved to Apple's Recently Deleted album, where they generally remain for 30 days before permanent removal. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple may synchronize that deletion across your devices.

Glano does not control Apple's iCloud services or their operation.

## 3. Local Storage and Backups

Glano stores certain app information locally on your iPhone, including scan results, preferences, and deletion history.

Some of this information may be included in your device backups, depending on your iOS backup settings. These backups are managed by Apple or the backup service you choose, not by Glano.

Temporary scan cache data is excluded from device backups.

Glano does not have access to your iCloud account or your device backups.

## 4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Glano may offer paid features or subscriptions.

All in-app purchases, payments, subscription management, and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

Glano does not collect or store your payment card details, Apple Account password, or payment information.

For information about purchases, billing, cancellations, or refunds, please refer to Apple's policies and support resources.

## 5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Glano schedules notifications locally on your device.

Glano does not use its own notification servers or send push notifications from a Glano server.

You can manage notification permissions at any time through your iPhone settings.

## 6. Support Communications

If you contact us at **support@glano.co**, we will receive the email address and message you choose to send.

We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support. Please avoid including sensitive personal or financial information in support messages.

Your email is handled through your email provider and our support email service.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Glano does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

The app uses Apple's built-in frameworks and services for features such as photo library access, image analysis, notifications, and in-app purchases.

Your use of Apple's services is subject to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

## 8. Data Security

Glano uses iOS-provided file protection for locally stored app information.

However, no method of electronic storage or security can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage you to keep your device and operating system updated.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Glano does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not operate a service that collects personal information from users.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its features change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
